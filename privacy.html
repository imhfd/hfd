Privacy and analytics

Updated 9 October 2026.

This website introduces the Inner Compass programme. Enrollment and payment take place on Skool, which has its own privacy terms.

Your choice

Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity are optional. Their scripts load after you accept analytics cookies. Declining keeps them blocked. You can change your choice using “Cookie preferences” in the website footer. Your preference is saved for up to 180 days in your browser.

What the analytics help us understand

Google Analytics measures page visits, campaign traffic, scroll depth, pricing and testimonial views, and Join clicks. A checkout event describes a handoff to Skool, not a completed purchase. Microsoft Clarity helps us understand clicks and scrolling through heatmaps and session recordings.

We do not send names, email addresses, form answers, payment details or passwords as analytics event parameters. Inputs and testimonials are masked for recordings. We keep only approved campaign tags in tracked URLs and omit other URL query parameters. The services also process technical information such as browser and device details and connection information according to their own policies.

Cookies and storage

After consent, Google Analytics may use first-party analytics cookies, and Clarity may use its analytics cookies. Advertising storage and advertising personalisation remain disabled in our configuration. The fixed launch deadline does not use cookies or browser storage and does not reset for individual visitors.

External services

The embedded programme video is provided by Gumlet. Playing or loading that player involves its service independently of optional analytics. The Join link takes you to Skool, which manages enrollment, accounts and payment.
